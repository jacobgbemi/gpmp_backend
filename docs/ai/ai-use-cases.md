# AI Use Cases

**Status:** AI use-case baseline  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-07

## Purpose

Identify useful AI applications while distinguishing low-risk assistance from high-risk automation.

## Use-Case Categories

### 1. Project Understanding

AI can help answer questions such as:

- What is the current project status?
- What are the major schedule variances?
- Which milestones are at risk?
- What changed since the previous reporting period?

### 2. Reporting Assistance

Potential capabilities:

- draft executive summaries;
- summarize reporting-period changes;
- convert structured metrics into narrative;
- identify notable variances;
- prepare first-draft commentary.

### 3. Schedule Analysis

Potential capabilities:

- identify unusual schedule patterns;
- explain critical-path changes;
- summarize delayed activities;
- identify activities requiring review;
- assist with schedule-quality checks.

AI recommendations should remain advisory unless explicitly approved by an authorized user.

### 4. Cost Analysis

Potential capabilities:

- summarize cost variances;
- identify unusual movements;
- explain trends from supplied data;
- draft cost-report commentary.

Financial calculations should preferably come from deterministic application logic rather than free-form model arithmetic.

### 5. Progress Analysis

AI may:

- summarize progress;
- compare current and previous periods;
- identify inconsistent narratives;
- draft progress commentary.

### 6. Risk Management

AI may assist with:

- risk identification;
- risk descriptions;
- grouping similar risks;
- drafting response options;
- summarizing risk exposure.

Risk ownership and acceptance remain human responsibilities.

### 7. Knowledge Assistant

AI can provide natural-language access to:

- project documentation;
- methodology;
- approved procedures;
- user guides;
- project records that the user is authorized to access.

## Risk Classification

| Risk | Example |
|---|---|
| Low | Summarization |
| Moderate | Variance explanation |
| High | Forecast recommendation |
| Very high | Autonomous financial/schedule change |

Higher-risk use cases require stronger validation and human approval.

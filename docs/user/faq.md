# Frequently Asked Questions

**Status:** User guide baseline  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-06

## What is GlintPM Private?

GlintPM Private is intended to support project controls and project management workflows, including project, schedule, cost, progress, reporting, and dashboard activities.

## Why can't I see a project?

You may not have permission to access it, the project may belong to another organization/workspace, or the project may be inactive. Contact an authorized administrator.

## Why can't I edit a record?

Your role may provide read-only access, or the record may be locked because of its lifecycle/status.

## Why does my report not match the dashboard?

Check the reporting period, filters, data refresh, schedule version, baseline, and cost/progress data used by each view.

## What is a baseline?

A baseline is an approved reference plan used to compare actual or forecast performance against the original or approved plan.

## What is a data date?

The data date is the point in time through which project status information has been updated for a reporting cycle.

## Why is my schedule variance high?

Possible causes include delayed activities, changed logic, changed calendars, baseline differences, incomplete progress updates, or forecast changes. Review the detailed schedule before drawing conclusions.

## Why is a cost variance negative or positive?

The meaning depends on the metric definition. Always check whether the application is showing budget-minus-actual, actual-minus-budget, or another defined calculation.

## Can I delete project data?

Deletion depends on permissions and data-governance rules. Some records may be archived rather than permanently deleted.

## How should I report a problem?

Provide the project, action, expected result, actual result, time of occurrence, and any non-sensitive error/request ID.

## Can I share my password with support?

**No.** Never share passwords, authentication tokens, API keys, or other secrets with support.

## What should I do if I suspect unauthorized access?

Stop sharing the affected account, notify the appropriate administrator/security contact, and follow the incident-response procedure.

## Why can't I import my data?

Check the supported file format, required fields, identifiers, dates, data types, and validation errors. Do not repeatedly import the same dataset until the cause is understood.

## Are the calculations automatically correct?

Calculations are based on the application's defined rules and input data. Users should verify important results against project controls procedures and source records.

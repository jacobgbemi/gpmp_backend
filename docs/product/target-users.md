# GlintPM Private --- Target Users

## 1. Overview

GlintPM Private is intended for organizations and professionals involved
in planning, controlling, delivering, and overseeing projects.

The primary target is expected to be **project-based organizations where
schedule, cost, progress, risk, and reporting information materially
affect project outcomes.**

The exact ideal customer profile should be validated through customer
discovery.

## 2. Potential Organization Types

Potential target organizations include:

-   Construction companies
-   Engineering and EPC companies
-   Oil and gas contractors
-   Energy and power companies
-   Infrastructure companies
-   Real-estate development organizations
-   Engineering consultancies
-   Project-management consultancies
-   Other organizations delivering complex projects

## 3. Primary User Groups

### Project Controls Manager

Needs:

-   Portfolio/project visibility
-   Consistent reporting
-   Control of planning and cost processes
-   Exception visibility
-   Accountability
-   Management reporting

### Project Planner / Scheduler

Needs:

-   Project schedule information
-   Baseline comparison
-   Progress updates
-   Schedule variance
-   Forecasting
-   Reporting
-   Integration with existing planning practices

### Cost Engineer

Needs:

-   Budget information
-   Actual costs
-   Forecasts
-   Cost variance
-   Change/variation visibility
-   Traceability

### Project Manager

Needs:

-   A consolidated view of project performance
-   Exceptions requiring action
-   Risks and issues
-   Schedule and cost status
-   Decisions and responsibilities

### Project Director / Executive

Needs:

-   High-level project/portfolio visibility
-   Exception reporting
-   Forecast information
-   Confidence in reported data
-   Decision-support information

### PMO / Management Office

Needs:

-   Standardized project processes
-   Reporting consistency
-   Portfolio visibility
-   Governance
-   Historical information

## 4. Secondary Users

Potential secondary users include:

-   Commercial managers
-   Quantity surveyors
-   Procurement teams
-   Finance teams
-   Risk managers
-   Document controllers
-   Project administrators
-   Senior management

Access should be controlled according to role and business need.

## 5. Economic Buyer

Potential economic buyers include:

-   Business owners
-   Managing directors
-   Project directors
-   Heads of project controls
-   PMO leaders
-   Operations leaders

The actual buyer may differ by organization size and structure.

## 6. User Environment

Users may work with:

-   Large project datasets
-   Multiple projects
-   Multiple teams
-   Existing scheduling software
-   Spreadsheets
-   PDFs and documents
-   Remote and site-based teams
-   Periodic reporting cycles

The product should therefore prioritize clarity, reliability,
traceability, and practical workflows.

## 7. Customer Qualification Questions

Before onboarding an organization, consider:

-   How many active projects exist?
-   What project types are delivered?
-   What planning software is used?
-   How are project costs tracked?
-   How is progress measured?
-   How frequently are reports produced?
-   What systems contain authoritative data?
-   Where does reconciliation occur?
-   What are the biggest reporting delays?
-   Who owns project controls?

## 8. Out-of-Scope Initial Audience

Unless validated by research, the first product should avoid trying to
optimize simultaneously for:

-   Personal task management
-   Generic consumer productivity
-   Social project management
-   Very small non-project businesses

The initial focus should remain on organizations where project controls
create meaningful operational value.

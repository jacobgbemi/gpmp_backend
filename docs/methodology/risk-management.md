# Risk Management

**Status:** Methodology baseline  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-07

## Purpose

Risk management identifies uncertainty that could affect project objectives and establishes responses before risks become issues where possible.

## Risk Process

```text
Identify
  ↓
Analyze
  ↓
Prioritize
  ↓
Respond
  ↓
Monitor
  ↓
Close
```

## Risk Record

Typical fields include:

- risk ID;
- description;
- cause;
- event;
- consequence;
- probability;
- impact;
- score;
- owner;
- response;
- target date;
- status.

## Risk Score

A simple illustrative model is:

```text
Risk Score = Probability × Impact
```

The actual scoring scale should be defined by project governance.

## Responses

Typical response strategies include:

### Threats

- Avoid
- Mitigate
- Transfer
- Accept

### Opportunities

- Exploit
- Enhance
- Share
- Accept

## Risk Monitoring

Monitor:

- risk status;
- triggers;
- response actions;
- residual exposure;
- emerging risks;
- overdue actions.

## Risk vs Issue

A **risk** is uncertain and may occur.

An **issue** has already occurred or is currently affecting the project.

Once a risk materializes, it may need to be managed through issue/change processes.
